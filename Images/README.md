# Images

Images made for this repository, kept as they came out so they can be reused: in tests, in other fixtures, or cut down for a particular use.

## Portraits

[`Portraits/`](Portraits/) holds portraits of fictional people in a dreamy gradient style, as 1024×1536 PNGs, unedited. The letter in each name is the look it was made with:

- A: three-quarter face, saturated orange background;
- B: backlit side profile, lilac-to-peach sky;
- C: glowing side profile, periwinkle-to-violet background.

The generator didn't always follow the look, and `portrait-03-C.png` faces right where the others face left.

They were made with ChatGPT image generation and are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). With thanks to [Novoda](https://www.novoda.com) for the image generation, and to [Jake Lee (@jakelee_art)](https://www.instagram.com/jakelee_art/), whose dreamy gradient portraits inspired the style.

Smaller copies, matched to names, are the portraits on Candy's organisational chart (`Repositories/Candy/Assets/portrait-*.jpg`).
