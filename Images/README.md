# Images

Images made for this repository, kept as they came out so they can be reused: in tests, in other fixtures, or cut down for a particular use.

## Portraits

[`Portraits/`](Portraits/) holds portraits of fictional people in a dreamy gradient style, as 1024×1536 PNGs, unedited. The letter in each name is the look it was made with:

- A: three-quarter face, saturated orange background;
- B: backlit side profile, lilac-to-peach sky;
- C: glowing side profile, periwinkle-to-violet background.

The generator didn't always follow the look, and `portrait-03-C.png` faces right where the others face left. `portrait-12.png` was made later, to replace `portrait-01-A.png` on the organisational chart, as 01 looked too much like 08.

They were made with ChatGPT image generation and are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). With thanks to [Novoda](https://www.novoda.com) for the image generation, and to [Jake Lee (@jakelee_art)](https://www.instagram.com/jakelee_art/), whose dreamy gradient portraits inspired the style.

Smaller copies, matched to names, are the portraits in the canvas fixture [`Fixtures/canvas/org-chart.canvas`](../Fixtures/canvas/) (`Fixtures/canvas/portraits/`).

## Field Notes and Coast

Photographs, and one pencil map, made for the TextBundle fixtures ([`Fixtures/textbundle/`](../Fixtures/textbundle/)), kept as they came out:

- [`Field Notes/`](Field%20Notes/): a heron at dawn, a fern frond, a mossy dry-stone wall (all JPEG), and a pencil map of a walk (PNG). 1,024 to 1,536 px.
- [`Coast/`](Coast/): a lighthouse at dusk (16:9), and twelve photographs of one week in the same small fishing town: the harbour, cliffs, beach huts, fishing boats, a market stall, a café table, the view from a train, rock pools, a lane of cottages, a sunset, a bookshop and the lighthouse at night. 1,024 to 1,672 px.

They show no real people or places, and no readable text. Like the portraits, they were made with ChatGPT image generation and are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
